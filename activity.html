Input format

Final answers must be in two significant figures. Large or small numbers may be typed as a raw value or in scientific notation.

Accepted forms: 2.5e10, 2.5 x 10^10, 2.5 * 10^10, 2.5×10^10, or the raw value 25000000000.

Negative answers are accepted where the expected value is negative, for example -2.5e10.

Each field is graded strictly against the correct two-significant-figure value.
